How values are built. SCMVAi = 100·|Vi|/|Zii| from the pre-fault state. SCR and WSCR use the network without IBRs at the POI buses. ESCR uses the network with IBRs at the MV buses, with interaction factors IFj←i = |ΔVj|/|ΔVi| from a +1 % voltage step; its ceiling SCMVAi/Prated,i is the value with all IF = 0. SDSCR uses the converter terminals with complex coupling weights (Wu 2018).
Model assumption. A generator in voltage control holds its bus voltage exactly, so it decouples the sites on either side of it. Real units regulate behind a step-up transformer with droop, so real interaction would be small but not zero.
Generator modes are from the network with IBRs. Sites pair the SCR, ESCR and SDSCR buses in the order the runner writes them. Voltage flags come from the run's voltage preset and are reported, not corrected. No threshold lines are drawn.
